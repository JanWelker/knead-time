# Community recipes

Want your dough on this list? Open a PR that adds one row below. The URL is
whatever the **Share** button copies — paste it as-is, the app decodes it.
For the **Name** column you can use either your full name (rendered as plain
text) or a GitHub handle prefixed with `@` (rendered as a link to your
profile, e.g. `@JanWelker`).

The `AVPN disciplinare (2024)` row is the association's own dough, read off
§2.1.2 of the [Disciplinare internazionale AVPN](https://www.pizzanapoletana.org/public/pdf/Disciplinare-2024-ITA.pdf)
at the middle of each range: per litre of water 50 g salt and 1.7 kg flour
(58.8 % hydration, 2.94 % salt), 280 g balls, fresh yeast, a direct dough
with no autolyse, fat or sugar, and 18 h at 20 °C, the middle of its 12–24 h.
It is set to keep the dough out of the fridge (`nf=1`), because the
disciplinare ferments at room temperature or 18–20 °C and never in one; without
that the app would run anything from 16 h up as a cold bulk. No flour is
stated: the spec names a strength (W 250–320), not a bag.
