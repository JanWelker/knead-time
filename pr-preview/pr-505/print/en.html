Weigh & prep
970 g Flour679 g Water
Weigh the flour and water (about 4 °C — chill it with ice cubes if your kitchen is warm) and mix them into a shaggy, lumpy mass with no dry flour left. No kneading yet; everything else waits for the mix. Before you mix, set aside two tablespoons (about 30 g) of the weighed water — the yeast is dissolved in it later.
15 min
Sat, Oct 31 · 02:04 PM
Autolyse rest
Cover the bowl and let the flour and water rest. The flour hydrates fully and gluten begins to form on its own, so the dough needs less kneading and stretches more easily later.
30 min

Mix dough
29 g Salt1.3 g Fresh yeast
Crumble the fresh yeast into the water you set aside and stir until it dissolves, then pour it over the rested dough. Work it in until it disappears, then scatter the salt evenly over the top — dry is fine, the moisture in the dough dissolves it — and work it in until you feel no more grains. Knead in the spiral mixer about 10 minutes until smooth and elastic, keeping the dough below 24 °C.
